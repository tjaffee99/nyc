# Military and institutional sites: researched building dates (sites2)

Files: `data/tables/known_building_dates.sites2.csv` (41 rows), `data/tables/notable_demolished.sites2.csv` (header only: 0 rows, see "Not found"). Downloads in `data/raw/sites2/`, scratch in `data/interim/sites2/` (`build.py` builds the CSV; `counts.py` the before/after counts). Outbound requests used the User-Agent `NYCThroughTime-research/0.1`.

## What the problem turned out to be
Inside these sites the built file's years are mostly (a) the lot's year stamped on every building (`lot_year`: Rikers 1933, Hart Island 1912, Snug Harbor 1900 / 1903, Fort Schuyler 1934), or (b) the placeholder 1931 spread (`heap_by`, Fort Wadsworth, Fort Hamilton), or (c) years that the pipeline moved *earlier* on the strength of the 1924 aerial photograph or neighbours. Fort Totten is not a problem site: the numbered buildings of its LPC report are already dated by `lpc` rows (checked, see below).

## Fort Wadsworth (25 rows)
* Source: National Park Service, *Cultural Landscape Report for Fort Wadsworth* (2023), https://www.npshistory.com/publications/gate/clr-fort-wadsworth.pdf (US government work, public domain). Text read: history chapters, Appendix A "Landscape Features Inventory" (building number, "Built YYYY", NR district list).
* Building 210 (Infantry Battalion Barracks, now park headquarters; OSM "Fort Wadsworth Visitor's Center and Museum"): begun 1929, completed two years later. Row start 1931. The built file had 1907 (aerial 1924 evidence P=0.91); it is not clear what the 1924 photograph saw (the early-1920s temporary wooden barracks stood nearby, CLR p.64) - not resolved.
* Buildings 111-114 (cruciform officers' housing on Mont Sec Avenue): inventory "Built 1890". Four footprints of identical area (274.7 m2) in a row at the OSM "Mont Sec House" point. Number-to-footprint match is by that row: estimated. The built file had 1916-1920.
* 20 footprints of about 455 m2, 11 m, three OSM parts, in the south-west corner (south of Batteries Ayers, Richmond, Barry): the CLR says the Navy demolished the Capehart housing (66 houses of the late 1950s) and replaced it with 32 multi-family units 1991-93. The built file dated them 1916-1932 (`heap_by`); the aerial photographs agree with the CLR (P(1924) is 0.00-0.16 and P(1951) 0.01-0.32 for all 20). Start 1992, estimated. Which footprints are the 32 units is inferred (size, shape, place); another 12 may exist outside my size filter.
* Found but not matched to footprints (no coordinates in the source): the inventory's Buildings 101-110, 117-119, 123 (housing and garages built 1932-39), 138 / 139 (water closets 1900 / 1891), 140 (mining casemate 1904), 147 (torpedo storage 1892-94, 3x18 bays), 148 (fire hose house), 211, 301-305 (1937-39 and 1900 bungalow), 309 / 352 (stables 1931 / 1921), 310 (1927), 404 / 406 (1939 / 1855-65), plus CLR text: stables 1921, parade ground 1925-27, stable fire 1930, hospital east of New York Avenue by 1895.
* Not found: outlines of the 66 Capehart houses (CLR fig. 2-63, NARA plan), the early-1920s temporary barracks, the wooden officers' quarters of c.1876-86 (about ten, Mont Sec Avenue), the trailer park of 1948.

## Fort Totten (1 row)
* Source: Landmarks Preservation Commission, Fort Totten Historic District designation report (LP-2040, 1999), https://s-media.nyc.gov/agencies/lpc/lp/2040.pdf (NYC government record). I parsed about 66 buildings with dates (guardhouse 1892, quartermaster storehouse 1897, barracks 322 / 323 / 325 1892-94, 405 1900, hospital 1906, twin dwellings 1905-06, theatre 1938-39 ...). For all of them the built file already has an `lpc` year within a year of the report, so no rows were written (checked by BIN: 65 of the 66 resolved footprints are `lpc`, start within a year of the report).
* Row: Building 619, communications bunker, "c. 1904" (secondary-buildings list); the built file had 1906 (`neighbours`). Estimated.
* The report says "some 80 structures" were built by the Quartermaster, "many replaced earlier wood-frame structures" and names the demolished frame dwellings near Sgt. Beers Avenue; no positions given, so no lost rows.

## Rikers Island (6 rows)
* Source: NYC Department of Correction history pages (via the NYC Historical Society mirror), https://correctionhistory.org/html/chronicl/nycdoc/html/jailist1.html and `jailst1a.html` (posted with the Department's permission; facts only). Opening years: George Motchan Detention Center 1971 (as the Correctional Institution for Women), Robert N. Davoren Center 1972 (as the Adolescent Reception and Detention Center), Anna M. Kross Center 1978, Otis Bantum 1985, Rose M. Singer 1988, George R. Vierno 1991. Also on those pages and not applied: North Infirmary Command (Rikers Hospital) 1932, James A. Thomas Center (House of Detention for Men) 1933, Eric M. Taylor Center (Correctional Institution for Men) 1964, expanded 1973, West Facility 1991 (Sprung structures).
* Each jail is matched to the footprint nearest its OSM-named centre (6-100 m): estimated. The rows for AMKC, GMDC, RNDC replace earlier map-bracket guesses of 1971 (`known_est`, `site_map:` rows) - the Department's dates are better; the lead should let sites2 win. OBCC's footprint has P(1951)=0.90 (a predecessor may have stood; flagged in the note).
* The other ~190 Rikers footprints keep the lot's 1933: no source ties them to a compound. A compound-level assignment (nearest named jail within ~150 m) would spread them over 1932-1991 but is inference; not done.

## Floyd Bennett Field (5 rows)
* Source: Wikipedia, Floyd Bennett Field (CC BY-SA, facts only; it cites the NPS Historic Structure Report 1981 and Cody 2009): hangars 1-8 begun late 1929, field completed 23 May 1931, Administration Building opened October 1931, Hangar B started 1942.
* Administration Building = the 16 m, 1,323 m2 footprint between the hangar groups (identified by height and place); hangars = the three large footprints on Hangar Row (hangar numbers not matched); all 1931, Hangar B 1943 (estimated). The built file had 1932-36 (`heap_about`).

## Miller Field (1 row), Sailors' Snug Harbor (2), Roosevelt Island (1)
* Miller Field North Hangar 1921: Wikipedia, "founded November 1919 and completed in 1921", four hangars when built; estimated (whether this standing hangar is of 1921 is assumed).
* Snug Harbor: Maintenance Building (Children's Museum main building) 1913; West Gatehouse 1880 - Wikipedia, Sailors' Snug Harbor, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailors'_Snug_Harbor. Buildings A-E 1833-1880 and the chapel 1856 are already `lpc`. The article says 23 buildings were demolished in 1950 and the complex once had 30-70 buildings; no list of them.
* Roosevelt Island: Bird S. Coler Hospital 1952 (Wikipedia, Coler Specialty Hospital): the 16,800 m2 footprint at the north tip was dated 1905 by the built file (`heap_by`). Strecker Laboratory (1892) agrees with its LPC year (no row).
