'''Crown Towers''' is a [[high-rise]] apartment building in [[New Haven]], [[Connecticut]] part of the New Haven Towers family of buildings. The building stands 222 ft (68 m) tall and is the [[List of tallest buildings in New Haven|eleventh-tallest building]] in the city, the  [[Modern architecture|modernist]] building was New Haven's tallest residential building until the construction of several new developments in Downtown New Haven.
