'''360 State Street''' is a {{convert|338|ft|m|adj=on}} residential [[skyscraper]] completed in 2010 in [[New Haven, Connecticut]]. It is the [[List of tallest buildings in New Haven|second-tallest building]] in the city, and the largest apartment building in the state.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://newyork.construction.com/features/archive/2009/06_C_360_State_Street.asp |title=360 State Street |work=New York Construction |access-date=4 June 2011}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last=Bass |first=Paul |url=http://www.newhavenindependent.org/index.php/archives/entry/dig_out_that_downturn/ |title=Dig Out That Downturn |work=New Haven Independent |date=1 December 2008 |access-date=2011-04-06}}</ref> DeSimone Consulting Engineers were the structural engineers on the building and it won the 2009 New York Construction – Top Project of the Year.<ref>{{Cite web|title = 360 State Street - DeSimone|url = http://www.de-simone.com/projects/project/360-state-street/|website = www.de-simone.com|access-date = 2015-12-16}}</ref>

==Description ==

The mixed-use [[Modern architecture|modernist]] building, includes 500 luxury apartments and {{convert|25000|sqft|abbr=on}} of retail space. Designated a "green" building by the [[US Green Building Council]] (USGBC), it is the first residential building in Connecticut to gain [[Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design]] (LEED) Platinum status and includes a rooftop garden and a pool as well as a corner "pocket park" that may be developed as a day care center in the future.<ref>{{cite web | author=Bruce Becker |url=http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/leading-the-way-in-green-design-connecticuts-first-leed-platinum-apartment-building-opens-leasing-office-87654877.html |title=Leading the Way in Green Design, Connecticut's First LEED Platinum Apartment Building |work=PRNewswire |date=15 March 2010 |access-date=3 April 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.downtownnewhaven.blogspot.com/2008/04/500-unit-shartenberg-mixed-use.html |title=Design New Haven: 500-unit Shartenberg Mixed-Use Development Progresses |work=Downtown New Haven |date=2009-10-21 |access-date=3 April 2013}}</ref> A full-scale food co-op occupies the building's ground floor.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.courant.com/2010/03/09/new-haven-apartment-tower-4700-a-month-penthouse/ |title=New Haven Apartment Tower: $4,700-A-Month Penthouse |work=Hartford Courant |date=2010-03-09 |access-date=2011-04-06}}</ref> 360 State was constructed on the site where [[Shartenberg's Department Store]] stood from 1915 to 1962.<ref>{{cite web|work=Emporis |url=http://www.emporis.com/application/?nav=building&lng=3&id=shartenbergsitetower-newhavenuen-ct-usa |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110604233145/http://www.emporis.com/application/?nav=building&lng=3&id=shartenbergsitetower-newhavenuen-ct-usa |url-status=usurped |archive-date=June 4, 2011 |title=360 State Street, New Haven, U.S.A. |access-date=3 April 2013}}</ref> The building allows pets except for the common areas of the 6th floor.

== Environmentally friendly features ==
* 360 State Street was Connecticut's first residence targeting LEED Platinum Certification 
* 1/2 the [[carbon footprint]] and utility bill of a conventional apartment 
* [[PureCell System|400 kW fuel cell]] on site produces renewable power <ref>[http://www.wtnh.com/dpp/news/new_haven_cty/New-Haven-360-state-street-gets-'green'-fuel-cell 360 State Street gets 'green' fuel cell ] News8 Wtnh Retrieved Friday, 28 May 2010</ref>
* Elevators recapture their own energy
* Electric-car charging stations
* Zipcars available 
* Bicycle storage 
* Built with recycled and local construction materials
* Recycling rooms on each floor
* Half-acre green roof with [[rainwater harvesting]] and irrigation system 
* High-efficiency lighting and occupancy sensors
* Demand-control ventilation
* Exhaust-heat energy recovery
* Location next to State Street train station provides direct access to New York. Nearby Union Station provides travel to [[Boston]], [[Hartford, Connecticut|Hartford]] and [[Washington, D.C.|Washington, DC]]
