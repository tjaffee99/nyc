The '''Connecticut Financial Center''' is the [[List of tallest buildings in New Haven|tallest building]] in [[New Haven]], [[Connecticut]], United States, and the sixth tallest building in the state.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://skyscraperpage.com/diagrams/?stateID=36 |title=Connecticut Skyscraper Diagram |publisher=SkyscraperPage.com |date= |accessdate=2022-05-02}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/connecticut-financial-center/9793|title=Connecticut Financial Center | website=     The Skyscraper Center| access-date=2017-06-12}}</ref> It was the first building in [[New Haven]] to be over 328 ft (100 m) tall. The 383 foot [[postmodern architecture|postmodern]] [[skyscraper]] was designed by the Toronto architectural firm Crang and Boake and completed in 1990. It is adjacent to New Haven City Hall facing the [[New Haven Green]] in [[Downtown New Haven]]. Among the current tenants of the building are [[United Illuminating]], [[Bank of America]], [[Merrill Lynch]], the U.S. Attorney’s Office, [[U.S. Bankruptcy Court]], and the Social Security Administration’s Office of Hearings and Appeals.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cityofnewhaven.com/uploads/157%20Church%20Street.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2010-03-17 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110605170533/http://www.cityofnewhaven.com/uploads/157%20Church%20Street.pdf |archivedate=2011-06-05 }}</ref> The CFC stands on the former site of the Powell Building, which was New Haven's first skyscraper.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.emporis.com/application/?nav=building&lng=3&id=149124 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110604233115/http://www.emporis.com/application/?nav=building&lng=3&id=149124 |url-status=usurped |archive-date=June 4, 2011 |title=Powell Building, New Haven &#124; 149124 |publisher=Emporis |date= |accessdate=2022-05-02}}</ref>
