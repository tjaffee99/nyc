'''McCormick Hall''' is a historic building on the campus of [[Hastings College]] in [[Hastings, Nebraska]], United States. It was built in 1883–1884, and designed in the [[Italianate architecture|Italianate architectural style]].<ref name="nrhpdoc">{{cite web|url={{NRHP url|id=75001086}}|title=National Register of Historic Places Inventory/Nomination: McCormick Hall |publisher=[[National Park Service]]|author= |date= |accessdate=March 12, 2019}} With {{NRHP url|id=75001086|photos=y|title=accompanying pictures}}</ref> It was the first building on campus, and it housed the departments of English, Journalism, Speech, Drama, Mathematics and Chemistry.<ref name="nrhpdoc"/> There was also a Presbyterian chapel on the second floor, later a theatre.<ref name="nrhpdoc"/> It has been listed on the [[National Register of Historic Places listings in Adams County, Nebraska|National Register of Historic Places]] since May 12, 1975.<ref name="nris"/>
