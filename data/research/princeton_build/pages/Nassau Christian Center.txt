The '''Nassau Christian Center''' is an [[Assemblies of God USA|Assemblies of God]] church in [[Princeton, New Jersey]] located at 26 [[Nassau Street (Princeton)|Nassau Street]].  It is housed in an historic church building built in 1868<ref>{{cite book|last1=Hageman|first1=John Frelinghuysen|title=History of Princeton and Its Institutions, Volume 2|date=1879|publisher=J.B. Lippincott & Company|pages=200–208|isbn=9780598745644 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=XzwVAAAAYAAJ&q=%22Second+Presbyterian+Church%22+Princeton&pg=PA201}}</ref> that was once home to Princeton's '''Second Presbyterian Church,''' later known as '''St. Andrew's Presbyterian.'''  The Christian Center was founded in 1978 and leased, purchasing in 1980, the then empty building from [[Nassau Presbyterian Church]], which had been formed by the merger of Second Presbyterian with First Presbyterian of Princeton in 1973.<ref>{{cite web|title=NCC History|url=http://www.nassauchristian.org/ncc/welcome/ncc-history/|website=Nassau Christian Center|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20140513100945/http://www.nassauchristian.org/ncc/welcome/ncc-history/|archivedate=2014-05-13}}</ref>
