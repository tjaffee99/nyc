'''Yeh College'''<ref>{{cite web |last1=Advancement Communications |title=Major gift from Jaimie and James Yeh ’87 names residential college and supports undergraduate expansion |url=https://www.princeton.edu/news/2022/05/11/major-gift-jaimie-and-james-yeh-87-names-residential-college-and-supports |website=Princeton University |publisher=Princeton University |access-date=11 May 2022 |ref=yeh}}</ref> (provisionally known as '''New College East''' until 2022, and '''Residential College 7''' until 2021) is the seventh [[Princeton University#Residential colleges|residential college]] at [[Princeton University]]. It opened at the beginning of the academic year in September 2022.<ref name="stateof2020" /> The construction of Yeh College increased the undergraduate student body population by 10 percent, or 500 students. It aims to be [[LEED Gold]] certified.<ref name="facilities">{{cite web |title=New Residential Colleges, including Perelman College |url=https://facilities.princeton.edu/projects/new-residential-colleges-including-perelman-college |website=Princeton University Facilities |publisher=Princeton University |access-date=28 June 2020 |archive-date=29 June 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200629144858/https://facilities.princeton.edu/projects/new-residential-colleges-including-perelman-college |url-status=dead }}</ref> [[Deborah Berke Partners]] are the architects of the new buildings.<ref name="roinj">{{cite web |last1=Strauss |first1=Eric |title=Perelman gift to fund new residential college at Princeton |url=https://www.roi-nj.com/2018/12/05/education/perelman-gift-to-fund-new-residential-college-at-princeton/ |website=ROI-NJ |publisher=ROI-NJ |access-date=28 June 2020}}</ref> A sister college, tentatively titled [[New College West]], was built adjacent to Yeh College, and shares the same dining facility.<ref name="facilities" /> New College West houses students displaced by the demolition of [[First College]], which is to be replaced with [[Hobson College]] in 2027.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-11-01|title=Home Page {{!}} Construction at Princeton|url=https://construction.princeton.edu/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211101154145/https://construction.princeton.edu/|url-status=dead|archive-date=2021-11-01|access-date=2021-11-01}}</ref>
