'''Princeton Cemetery''' is located in [[Princeton, New Jersey]], United States.<ref name=online>{{cite web |url=http://www.princetonol.com/groups/cemetery/ |title=The Princeton Cemetery |access-date=2007-08-26 |publisher=Princeton Online |archive-date=2007-08-26 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070826173720/http://www.princetonol.com/groups/cemetery/ |url-status=live }}</ref> It is owned by the [[Nassau Presbyterian Church]].<ref>{{cite book |last=Sarapin |first=Janice Kohl |title=Old Burial Grounds of New Jersey |year=2002 |publisher=[[Rutgers University Press]] |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=uDfIJt5RFWgC&pg=PP1 |isbn=0813521114 |access-date=2020-10-18 |archive-date=2023-08-18 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230818033939/https://books.google.com/books?id=uDfIJt5RFWgC&pg=PP1 |url-status=live }}</ref> In his 1878 history of Princeton, New Jersey, John F. Hageman refers to the cemetery as "The [[Westminster Abbey]] of the United States."<ref name=online/><ref name=nyt1/>
