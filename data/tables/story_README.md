# story.csv - the guided tour ("story mode")
Columns: `id,year,year_to,lon,lat,zoom,pitch,bearing,seconds,title,text,source_url` (UTF-8, RFC 4180 quoting). One row per stop, in chronological order.
The viewer flies to (lon, lat) at zoom/pitch/bearing, sets the year (running to `year_to` if given, at most 40 years, and the next stop must start at or after it), shows title (max 48) and text (max 230 for the first 106 stops, 200 for the 69 added in the round of 2026-11-11), and rotates 10 degrees for `seconds` (8-20; new short stops 7-11). The viewer dwells 62 % of `seconds` and skips a stop that lies outside the map.
Facts and coordinates come from the project's tables: `events.csv` and `events_counties.csv` (dated events, coordinates, source_url), `landmarks_*.csv` (coordinates and lifespans, year_source_url) and `early_nyc.csv` (Lenape sites, Bolton / Parker). Stops taken from a landmark must fall inside its start_year-end_year.
Two stops rest on pages opened for the tour: the 1863 draft riots and the 1975 fiscal crisis (Wikipedia, opened 2026-11); the closing stop uses the population table's census source.
To add a stop: pick the row's year, take lon/lat from the table that holds the place, write one or two plain present-tense sentences that are true of what the map shows at that year, add the source URL, and insert the row in year order.
Then re-check: header exact, ids unique, years non-decreasing, coordinates inside the map's box (-75.37, 39.47, -71.85, 42.20), zoom 11-17, pitch 45-70, seconds 8-20 (new stops 7-11), text <= 230 (new stops <= 200), 170-180 rows, seconds sum 1,850-2,000; each `year_to` at most 40 years after `year` and not after the next stop's `year`.
Growth stops (`year_to`): a place coming into being runs from a few years before its first buildings to when it is there. Each was checked against `data/interim/nyc_buildings.parquet` and `county_buildings.parquet` (buildings with a start year in the span within ~500 m of the stop, 2026-11-11): Manhattan 1625-30 (37), Bronck's land 1638-40 (5), New Amsterdam 1640-46 (68), Breuckelen 1646-52 (15), the Wall 1653-55 (39), the town 1656-60 (84), Oude Dorp 1661-64 (7), the Navy Yard 1864-69 (59) and 1940-44 (57), the western Bronx 1872-78 (12), University Heights 1900-01 (10), Stuyvesant Town 1944-47 (132), Co-op City 1965-70 (184), Hudson Yards 2017-19 (25); older ones: Els uptown 1878-83, Brooklyn 1887-94, the race for the sky 1929-30, Lincoln Center 1959-62, Battery Park City 1976-83, September 11 (2001.62-2001.8, the towers vanish) and the fire of 1776 (buildings vanish). Places without a growth span because the map has no buildings there in the span: Pavonia, Bergen, Newark (1666), Elizabethtown, Tappan, Kingsbridge, Philipsburg, the outer towns (New Haven, Southampton, Southold, Kingston), Levittown (Nassau is not in the building files read; add `year_to` 1951.5 if the build shows houses appearing), Jersey City 1804 (its buildings start in 1830), Llewellyn Park (13 buildings 1857-75, but the Central Park stops follow within a year).
Added 2026-11-11 (69 stops, ids in the file): the new landmarks (High Line 1934, Farley 1914 and Moynihan 2021, Brooklyn Tower, Riverbank, Dakota, Carnegie Hall, Plaza, Cloisters, TWA, Oculus, Kingsbridge Armory, Apollo; facts from `viewer/src/landmarks/nyc-more-*.ts` and `data/research/landmarks_more_*.md`), the Navy Yard, Newark's Central Ward before and after its clearance (`data/manual/gapfill/cleared_districts.geojson`), Port Elizabeth (`data/manual/ports`), Levittown, counties around the city (`nj-inner.ts`, `ny-hudson.ts`, `events_counties.csv`) and the outer counties (`nj-outer.ts`, `ny-long-island.ts`, `ct.ts`, `places_outer.csv`, `routes_outer.csv`). Carnegie Hall, the Communipaw terminal and the Poughkeepsie bridge sit at 1894.6-1894.7 (after the Brooklyn span, which they would otherwise cut) and are worded "has stood since". Wikipedia pages for the battles of White Plains, Trenton and Princeton, the Military Academy, the Morris Canal, Branch Brook Park and others were not opened in this round; their facts come from the landmark notes and tables or are well-known dates.
Total: 175 stops, 1,927 seconds written; at 62 % that is 1,195 s plus about 2.6 s of flight per stop (455 s): roughly 27.5 minutes.
Not checked on screen: the captions were written against the tables, not against a rendered map; eyeball the stops where a caption names something drawn (Latting Observatory, Church in the Fort, Lincoln Center).
The generator was a throwaway script; the CSV is the source of truth. Edit it directly.
